Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Gizmoddity is a small faceless video channel about clever gadgets, on YouTube (@gizmoddity) and TikTok (@gizmoddity). This policy covers this website (lirakza.github.io/gizmoddity) and the Gizmoddity publishing app, which the channel owner uses to post Gizmoddity's own videos to Gizmoddity's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Visitors to this website

  • We don't collect, store or sell any personal information, and there are no forms or accounts.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Product images load from the store's servers (AliExpress), which may see your IP address in the same way as any web request.

Affiliate links

The "Get it" buttons are affiliate links to third-party stores (AliExpress and, where shown, Amazon). When you click one, you leave this site, and the store may set its own cookies to credit the referral. We may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. The store's own privacy policy applies there.

The Gizmoddity publishing app (TikTok and YouTube)

  • The app is used only by the channel owner, to upload Gizmoddity videos and their captions to the Gizmoddity accounts.
  • With the owner's permission, it receives access tokens from TikTok and Google (YouTube). These are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to publish and check the status of our own posts.
  • It may read basic profile information (such as the account name) and the posting status of our own videos. It doesn't access other users' data, messages, followers' personal details or contacts.
  • No data from the app is sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings. Revoking it makes the stored tokens stop working.

Children

This site and the channel aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
